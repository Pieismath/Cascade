## Description

Cascade turns printed piano sheet music into a falling-note lesson you can watch, slow down, and play along with. Scan a page, and a minute later the notes are raining down onto the keys with real piano sound.

SCAN YOUR MUSIC
• Point your camera at printed sheet music, or choose photos or a PDF
• Every page becomes falling notes, ready to practice or turn into a video
• See it work first: four sample scans convert instantly, free
• Your first scan is free. More come in scan packs that never expire, with no subscription

OR BRING A FILE — FREE AND UNLIMITED
• Import MusicXML (.musicxml, .mxl, .xml) from MuseScore, Finale, Sibelius, and other notation apps
• Or start with nine free pieces, from Twinkle Twinkle to Für Elise, a Chopin nocturne, and Flight of the Bumblebee

PRACTICE YOUR WAY
• Slow down to a quarter speed or speed up to double, and every note stays in tune
• Loop the bar that's giving you trouble
• Practice one hand at a time
• Listen Mode, for hearing a piece before you play it
• Play along on the on-screen keyboard, or plug in a MIDI keyboard for note-by-note feedback

MAKE A VIDEO
• Export any piece as a 1080p, 60 fps falling-notes video with piano audio
• Save it to Photos or share it anywhere, ready to post
• Seven color themes, or build your own

KEEP IT GOING
• Streaks, a practice calendar, and 20 awards to earn
• A gentle daily reminder, if you want one

No ads. No subscription. Everything except scanning works offline, and scanning uses Sign in with Apple without sharing your name or email.

Scanning works best with clearly printed piano scores.

## App Review notes

Cascade converts sheet music to interactive piano lessons and videos.

TESTING WITHOUT SCANNING: scroll down the Home screen to "Starter pieces" (nine public-domain pieces that work fully offline). Tap one to open it in the player; the film icon in the player exports a video. Above it, "See a scan" has four bundled sample scans (Für Elise opening, Minuet in G, Ode to Joy, Twinkle Twinkle) that run the whole scan → preview → video flow instantly with a prepared transcription, no sign-in or network needed; the app labels them as samples.

TESTING SCANNING: on Home tap the ••• button on the Scan tile → "Choose a PDF or images" and pick the attached sample PDF (or tap the Scan tile itself and photograph any printed piano score), then tap "Continue with Apple" (no name or email requested) and "Make falling notes". Each Apple account receives 1 free scan; further scans are sold as consumable in-app purchases (10 Scans, 25 Scans). Recognition takes roughly 30 to 90 seconds and needs a network connection. The images are processed by our server and discarded.

IN-APP PURCHASES: consumables are redeemed server-side after verifying Apple's signed transaction; the balance and scan packs are shown on the scan screen when needed and always in Home → Settings (gear icon), which also has Restore purchases.

ACCOUNT DELETION: Home → Settings (gear icon) → Delete account (visible once signed in).

PERMISSIONS: Camera is requested only when opening the document scanner. Photo library add-only access is requested only if the user chooses "Save Video" in the share sheet. Notifications are optional practice reminders.

No third-party analytics or ads are used.
