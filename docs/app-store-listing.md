## Description

Cascade turns any piano sheet music into an interactive falling-note lesson, the way you have seen on YouTube, right on your iPhone or iPad.

SCAN OR IMPORT ANYTHING
• Photograph printed sheet music with the built-in document scanner
• Import PDFs or photos from Files
• Open MusicXML files (.musicxml, .xml, .mxl) from MuseScore, Finale, Sibelius, or any notation app
• Start immediately with the bundled starter library: Twinkle Twinkle, Ode to Joy, Für Elise, Flight of the Bumblebee, a Chopin Nocturne and more

LEARN AT YOUR OWN PACE
• Notes fall onto an 88-key piano in time with real piano sound
• Slow down to 25% without changing pitch
• Practice one hand at a time
• Loop tricky sections until they stick
• Listen Mode to study a piece before you play it
• Play along on the on-screen keyboard or connect a MIDI keyboard and get note-by-note accuracy feedback

MAKE VIDEOS
• Export any piece as a 1080p 60fps falling-note video with audio, straight from a scan or from the player
• Share to Photos, Messages, or anywhere else
• Seven color themes plus custom colors

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
• Practice calendar, streaks, and accuracy history
• Achievements and shareable progress cards
• Optional daily practice reminders

Scanning uses a secure cloud service to read the notation. Sign in with Apple to get free scans; we never see your name or email. Everything else works fully offline.

## App Review notes

Cascade converts sheet music to interactive piano lessons and videos.

TESTING WITHOUT SCANNING: the Add tab includes a free starter library (eight pieces) that works fully offline. Tap any of them, then use the Play tab. The export button (share icon) in the player renders a video.

TESTING SCANNING: on the Add tab tap "Scan Sheet Music", sign in with Apple (no name or email requested), then import the attached sample PDF or photograph any printed piano score. Each Apple account receives 5 free scans. Recognition takes roughly 30 to 90 seconds and needs a network connection. The images are processed by our server and discarded.

ACCOUNT DELETION: Scan Sheet Music → gear icon → Delete Account.

PERMISSIONS: Camera is requested only when opening the document scanner. Photo library add-only access is requested only if the user chooses "Save Video" in the share sheet. Notifications are optional practice reminders.

No third-party analytics or ads are used.
